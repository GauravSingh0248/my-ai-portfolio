# MY PHILOSOPHY

## LEARN. BUILD. BREAK. IMPROVE.

### The mindset behind my journey.

I believe the best way to understand technology is to **build with it**.

Reading documentation and watching tutorials can teach you the fundamentals, but building something forces you to confront the parts that tutorials don't show you—the bugs, edge cases, architectural decisions, failures, and unexpected problems.

I've learned that breaking something isn't necessarily a setback.

Sometimes, it's the fastest way to understand how it works.

So my approach is simple:

**Learn something.**

**Build something with it.**

**Break it.**

**Understand why it broke.**

**Improve it.**

**Build again.**

I'm still at the beginning of the journey, and there's a lot more I want to learn, build, and experiment with.
